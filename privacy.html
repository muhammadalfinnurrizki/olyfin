Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

This policy explains what data Olyfin uses and why.

Data we use

  • Basic TikTok profile info (display name, avatar, open ID) to show which account is connected.
  • Access tokens issued by TikTok, used only to upload videos you choose.
  • Videos and captions you select for upload.

How we use it

Data is used only to upload your own videos to your TikTok account. We do not sell or share your data with third parties and do not use it for advertising.

Storage and deletion

Tokens and files are stored on a private server and deleted after upload or when you disconnect. You can request deletion at any time by contacting us, and you can revoke access in your TikTok app settings.
